Investment Tracker

A private record of your Indian investments that lives on your own PC or Android device.

Privacy policyTerms of service

What it does

  • Tracks mutual funds, shares, NPS, EPF and PPF accounts, Sovereign Gold Bonds, fixed deposits and life insurance policies: every purchase, sale, dividend, interest credit and premium, what the holdings are worth today and how they have performed (XIRR, realised and unrealised gains).
  • Fetches daily prices from public sources: mutual fund NAVs from mfapi.in (AMFI data), share prices and exchange rates from Yahoo Finance, NPS scheme NAVs from NPS Trust, gold bond prices from the NSE.
  • Imports a CAMS / KFintech consolidated account statement, keeps SIP schedules, prints or saves reports as PDF, and works out capital gains and the estimated tax for a financial year under the Indian rules.
  • Keeps everything in one encrypted file (AES-GCM, key derived from your password with scrypt) on the device. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising.

Backup to your own cloud storage (optional)

You can connect the app to your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox account so that the encrypted file is backed up there after every change and can be restored on another device. The app asks only for access to the folder it creates itself: in Google Drive the permission is drive.file, which lets the app see and change only files it created. The file is encrypted before it leaves the device, so the cloud never holds readable data, and the sign-in tokens stay on the device. You can disconnect in the app at any time and revoke the access in your Google account's third-party access page.
